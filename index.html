About the Chess Club

I originally started the chess club in S1 because I wanted to give people a place where they could play and enjoy chess. However, I quickly realised I was not running it in the best way, and only a few people were interested in coming. Eventually, I stopped going, especially when the teacher started allowing other people to use the club for different activities. Since this was right before moving into S2, I decided that instead of giving up on the idea, I would start it again the following year and do it properly. Now, the chess club meets in Room 2-043 on Thursdays and Fridays. We have chess boards available, but you can also bring your own chess set if you want. You can play against whoever you want, whether that is a friend, someone you have never played before, or even play online. If you do not know how to play chess, that is completely fine because I am happy to teach you. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced player, everyone is welcome.

Why Chess? Chess is more than just a game. It can help you develop your ability to think ahead, plan your moves and make strategic decisions. It also requires concentration, patience and problem solving. Playing a challenging game can even be mentally tiring because your brain is constantly thinking about different possibilities.

A Little About Me I started playing chess when I was five years old, and I still play every day for at least 30 minutes. I enjoy practising, improving my skills and challenging myself to find better moves. I also aim to get at least five checkmates every day when I play. I have also made a chess clock for the club, so if you want to play a proper timed game, you can use it. Whether you are here to learn, practise, compete with your friends or simply have fun, there is a place for you at the chess club.

Use the Chess Clock button on the home screen to jump straight into the clock and start a timed game with a friend.
